## Cloudflare

All requests to this website pass through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which protects the site against attacks and delivers it faster. Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical request data for this. Our legitimate interest is a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: [cloudflare.com/privacypolicy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Cookies and local storage

- **NEXT_LOCALE**: a technically necessary cookie that remembers the language you chose (German or English). It is deleted when you close your browser.
- **Theme**: your choice of light or dark mode is saved in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

No other cookies are set for visitors. Because both are strictly necessary for the functions you use, no consent is required (§ 165(3) TKG 2021).

## Fonts

The fonts are served from our own server. Your browser does not connect to Google Fonts or any other font provider.

## Sponsor inquiry form

If you send an inquiry through the media kit, we store the company, contact name, email address, budget range and message you enter, together with the language and the time of the inquiry. We use this data only to answer your inquiry and to prepare a possible collaboration (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your IP address is not stored with the inquiry.

Inquiries are kept in our database until they are handled. Inquiries that are completed or marked as spam are deleted automatically 12 months after their last change. You can ask for your inquiry to be deleted earlier at any time.

**Spam protection:**

- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that the form is sent by a person, not a bot. For this, Cloudflare processes technical data about your browser and device. Our legitimate interest is protecting the form against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information: [cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).
- **Rate limit:** to limit the number of inquiries per sender, a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself) is kept for one hour and then deleted automatically.

## Twitch and YouTube videos

The streams and videos on this website are only loaded from Twitch or YouTube once you click play. Until then, only a preview image from our own server is shown and no data is sent to these platforms.

After you click play, the player of the respective platform is loaded and your IP address and technical data are sent to it:

- **Twitch** (Twitch Interactive, Inc., USA): [twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice](https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice/)
- **YouTube** (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland), in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

The legal basis is your action of starting the video (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
